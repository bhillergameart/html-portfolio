My Favorite Films of All Time

A List of Ben Hiller's Favorite Movies

Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back

Star Wars Episode V features the first live action appearance of my favorite Star Wars Character, Boba Fett.
It is a cinematic masterpiece in which the heroes do not win, but are resolved to rally and continue fighting for what is right.

Star Wars Episode VI: The Return of the Jedi

Star Wars Episode VI features the second live action appearance of my favorite Star Wars Character, Boba Fett.
It also showcased the climactic end of the original Star Wars Saga, where good triumphed over the ultimate evil in the Galaxy.

Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope

While Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope does not feature a live action, or rather any, appearance of my favorite Star Wars Character, Boba Fett, it is the begining of a perfect trilogy of films that made huge leaps in visual effects and epic storytelling.
